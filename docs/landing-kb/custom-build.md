# Custom build

The off-the-shelf agent covers the standardisable ~80% of use cases. The
Custom-build engagement is for the rest — when you need more than the shipped
product.

## When you need it

- Bespoke conversation flows on your own stack
- Integrations we don't ship by default: your CRM, telephony, helpdesk, internal
  APIs or tools, a custom auth gateway
- Your own LLM instead of the default
- Your own UI on top of the chat experience

## How it works

- Scoped as a fixed-scope, 4–6 week build
- Starts with a 30-minute audit that scopes exactly what you'd need wired up
- Trained on your knowledge and your voice
- Credentials are encrypted in your isolated tenant
- You keep the code — includes runbooks and handover, with an optional retainer

If you outgrow the product later, you walk away with everything that was shipped.
