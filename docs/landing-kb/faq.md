# Frequently asked questions

## Is this just a chat widget?

No. The chat answers your website. Behind it, we automate your repetitive work
and pull your numbers into one live view — built around your own tools, and
scoped after the free 30-minute audit.

On its own the chat greets every visitor, does the job its role calls for
(qualify a lead, answer a sales question, resolve a support ticket, walk a new
user through setup), saves the conversation, and pushes the lead into your
dashboard.

## What does the agent actually do?

It greets every visitor, does the job its role calls for — qualify a lead,
answer a sales question, resolve a support ticket, walk a new user through
setup — saves the conversation, and pushes the lead into your dashboard. You
see every transcript. Nobody watches a queue.

## What do the plans cost?

Free is €0 — 1 agent, 250 conversation credits a month, no card. Starter is
€9/month (1 agent, 2,500 credits), Growth €19/month (up to 5 agents, 10,000),
Operator €39/month (up to 5 agents, 25,000, best rate per credit and the most
expensive plan we sell).

Every feature is on at every tier, and every paid plan cancels anytime with no
lock-in. Need more headroom? Buy a top-up pack or move up a plan; nothing about
your setup changes. Build work has no list price — it is quoted after the free
audit.

## Does the chat answer in Greek?

Yes — and in English, and in most languages a visitor writes in. The agent
mirrors the visitor's language automatically: someone who opens the chat in
Greek gets Greek back, with no setup and no separate agent per language. Your
knowledge base can stay in English and it will still answer Greek questions
from it. Where exact Greek wording matters — a service name, a legal phrase —
put that wording in the knowledge base and the agent will use it.

## Does it connect to my existing stack?

Every plan ships the agent itself — the chat, the knowledge base, transcripts,
and lead routing into your dashboard. Wiring it into your CRM, helpdesk or
internal APIs is custom build work: fixed scope, credentials encrypted in your
own tenant, and you keep the code. The free 30-minute audit is where that gets
scoped.

## Is there a free trial?

Better — there is a permanent free tier: 1 agent, 250 conversation credits a
month, every feature on, no card. Put the agent on your own site with your own
content before paying anything. It does not expire; when you outgrow 250 credits
you move to Starter at €9/month.

Separately, the 30-minute audit for build work is free, and ends in a written
fixed-scope proposal within 48 hours, yours to keep either way.

## When do I need a custom build instead of the off-the-shelf agent?

When you need bespoke flows, integrations we don't ship by default (an internal
CRM, a custom auth gateway, your own LLM), or your own UI on top of the chat.
The standard agent covers about 80% of what people need; we build the rest —
fixed scope, and you keep the code.

## Are we locked in?

No. Cancel any month — no minimums, no exit fees. Builds are fixed-scope and you
keep the code, so if you outgrow us you walk away with everything we shipped.

## How do conversation credits work?

Each visitor message and each agent reply costs conversation credits. On the
fast default model that is 1 credit per message; smarter models cost more,
because they cost us more (you pick the model per agent and can change it
anytime). Your monthly allowance resets on renewal. Top-up credits you paid for
roll over until used.

## How fast can I go live?

About a minute: pick a role, upload your knowledge, paste one script tag.

## How do I talk to a human?

Three ways: book the free 30-minute audit at flowstack.run/audit (a human runs
it), email hello@flowstack.run, or ask this chat for a human — the team is
notified and someone picks the chat up.
