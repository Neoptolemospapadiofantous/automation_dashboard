# What Flowstack is

Flowstack provides AI agents for sales, support, lead qualification and
onboarding — and bespoke automation when off-the-shelf isn't enough.

An agent is a chat experience you embed on your website. It greets every
visitor instantly, takes the action your chosen role needs (qualify a lead,
answer a sales question, resolve a first-line support ticket, or walk a new
user through onboarding), captures the whole conversation, and pushes qualified
leads into a real-time dashboard. You see every transcript without anyone
watching a queue.

Setup is fast: one agent goes live in about 60 seconds. You upload your own
knowledge (docs and FAQs), and the agent answers from what you actually ship
rather than generic filler. Pricing starts at €99/month and you can cancel
anytime — there is no lock-in.

The off-the-shelf agent covers the standardisable ~80% of use cases. When you
need bespoke flows, integrations we don't ship by default, your own LLM, or
your own UI, that's the Custom-build engagement.
