## Why we're different — and win

1. **Talk *and* do, in one agent.** The chatbot pack can't act; the ops pack
   can't reason. We decide mid-conversation to fire a real workflow and use the
   result. *(The [[phase-16-automations]] tool-call — already built.)*

2. **n8n as the hands = unbounded automation we didn't have to build.** 400+
   integrations, self-hostable, a real engine — vs. everyone else's capped or
   proprietary builders (Chatbase quotas, GHL's fiddly canvas, Voiceflow's
   Pipedream).

3. **Honest, seat-free, usage-based pricing.** Not per-seat (Crisp/GHL/
   Voiceflow), not per-resolution-that-punishes-success (Fin), not opaque
   message tiers. One credit ledger; top-ups roll over.

4. **A product, not a toolkit.** Multitenancy, draft→publish config, human
   handoff, lead capture, audit trails, branded widget — in the box. Configure,
   don't assemble (the key wedge vs. Voiceflow).

5. **The data layer is the long moat.** Every run + turn is audited
   (`automation_runs`, transcripts) — the seed of analytics & suggested
   automations we sell back. Bolt-on-AI competitors can't reconstruct it.

## Honest exposure (own it before they ask)

- **RAG is commoditized** — our defensibility lives in *act + data*, not retrieval.
- **GHL's switching cost is real** — we win *new* / AI-first operators, not rip-and-replace.
- **Voiceflow is better-funded** with mature voice — we win on *outcome over toolkit*, not feature count.
- **n8n is a dependency** — the Actions UI must hide its complexity.
- **Distribution is the gap** — incumbents have galleries + SEO; we need an acquisition motion.
